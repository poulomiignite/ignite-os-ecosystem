# Writing Preferences — Poulomi Basu (pbasu@weignitegrowth.com)

These apply to ALL copywriting done in this session: LinkedIn comments and
posts, captions, emails, marketing copy, etc. The goal is to sound human, not
like AI. Avoid the formulaic "insight" templates below.

## Banned phrases / negative keywords

Never use any of these constructions (or close variants):

### 1. "Quietly" + verb
- "quietly becoming"
- "quietly doing"
- "quietly happening"
- "quietly dies"
- Any "quietly [verb]" / "X is quietly [verb]-ing Y" pattern.

### 2. "Where the real ___ happens/lives"
- "where the real work lives"
- "where the real work happens"
- "where the real thing happens"
- Any "X is where the real ___ happens/lives" template.

### 3. "The line between X and Y is where ___"
- "the line between curious and committed is where..."
- Any "the line between X and Y is where [something] happens" template.

### 4. "The gap between X and Y"
- "the gap between the team you have and the team that's possible"
- Any "the gap between X and Y" construction.

## General principle
Avoid AI-tell contrast/revelation formulas: setup-then-twist clichés, faux-profound
one-liners, and manufactured "insight" framings. Write plainly and specifically.
When in doubt, cut the clever line.
